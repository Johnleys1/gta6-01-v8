Aurora Falls — a 3D open-world crime game in a snowbound mountain city under the aurora. Play Kai, getaway driver, with a crew of three (Maya, Dario, June) across 8 staged missions against THE WARDEN, the resort magnate who owns the mountain. Five districts — Neon Downtown, Old Mine Town, Ski Resort, Frozen Lake, The Pass — with ice-road driving, blizzards, AFPD dispatch and the NORTHERN-1 helicopter, roadblocks, spike strips, RAM/PIT units, a 4-weapon wheel, exploding cars, garage upgrades, district standing, street races, taxi jobs, saves and 3 radio stations.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
